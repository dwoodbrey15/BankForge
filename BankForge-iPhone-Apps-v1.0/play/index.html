Rules & offline setup

The first three rolls are safe: a seven adds 70 and other rolls add their sum. After that, seven ends the round; doubles double the pot; other rolls add their sum. Banking adds the whole pot to that player’s score.

Open once online and wait for “Offline ready”, then use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. The installed app works offline. Keep exported backups of important networks.

Network actions use the checkpoint’s sampling rule and the desktop’s fixed, proven game-rule overrides. These do not run Monte Carlo search. The displayed percentages are the raw network probabilities.
